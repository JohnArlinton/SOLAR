## Flow Power first-call ask script
- We own a Victorian C&I rooftop portfolio (first site 0.5–2 MWp PV with 1–4 MWh BESS); we hold the NMI interval data and the DNSP export figure.
- We need: (1) an export offtake path with price, term, and settlement terms; (2) flexibility management (arbitrage + FCAS) across the portfolio.
- We are NOT asking you to buy the asset or the roof — HEJ owns the PV and BESS; a partner handles the market interface on the first project.
- Close with: what do you need from us to issue indicative terms, and on what timeline.
