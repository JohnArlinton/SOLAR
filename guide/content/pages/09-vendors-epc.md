## Summary
Procure equipment and EPC on compliance and warranty strength, not headline price. Vendor and installer names are examples for competing proposals, not endorsements. Buy via Australian distributors on the first project for warranty, stock, and support.

## Installer shortlist (competing proposals, not endorsements)
- Energus (100+ MW delivered, C&I specialist) — see `market-contacts` for link.
- Choice Energy (C&I solar + procurement).
- Betta Value / BVR Energy (REC + EPC).
- Kratos Energy (100 kW commercial package).
- Seek at least three competing proposals; never sole-source the pilot.

## Requirement bar (non-negotiable)
- Registered Electrical Contractor (REC) + SAA-accredited designer and installer.
- Proven C&I EPC track record plus DNSP connection and BESS experience.
- Energy Safe Victoria compliance; CER/SRES compliance for certificates.

## PV hardware (buy via Australian distributors first project)
- Jinko, LONGi (Hi-MO 9 660 W, about 2.7 m² per panel), Trina, Canadian Solar.
- Distributor stock first project: local warranty handling, replacement stock, and engineering support beat a small direct-import saving.

## Prices are PLANNING RANGES — treat with caution
| Item | Planning range (A$, installed unless noted) |
|---|---|
| PV 20 kW–1 MW | Distributor quotes per site; model a range, not a point |
| PV 1 MW conservative | A$0.9–1.2m installed |
| BESS 2 MWh | A$1.4–2.4m installed |

- Factory-only battery prices quoted in US$20–70k are misleading: they exclude shipping, duties, installation, protection, EMS integration, commissioning, and warranty localisation. Never put a factory-only figure in the model. See `economics`.

## Certification layers (authoritative sequence)
1. REC and licensed electrician complete the build → Certificate of Electrical Safety (Energy Safe Victoria).
2. SAA-accredited designer and installer sign off; CER/SRES paperwork for certificates.
3. DNSP commissioning approval and witness tests before energisation.
4. BESS extras: fire separation sign-off, EMS integration test, insurer acceptance.

## EPC essentials (8 items)
1. Standards compliance path (AS/NZS 5033/5139/4777)
2. Structural and roof-protection method
3. Fire separation and compound layout
4. Protection settings per connection offer
5. Metering and EMS integration
6. Testing and witness-test readiness
7. Warranty capture and spares
8. Augmentation access and O&M handover

## Open decisions
- Score EPC bids against all 8 essentials, not price alone.
