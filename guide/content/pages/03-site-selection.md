## Summary
Due diligence runs as three parallel tracks — property, tenant, energy — converging in the financial model, then PPA/connection/approvals, then an Energy Offtake Strategy step, then one GO/NO-GO gate. Five investment gates decide every site; nothing closes on assumptions.

## Parallel tracks (not a sequential list)
- Property track: roof size/age, structure + remaining life, planning route.
- Tenant track: 12 months metered interval load (8 data requests), Tier-1 offtake, lease term vs 20–30 yr asset.
- Energy track: SunSPOT + shading, DNSP enquiry, PV/BESS sizing from load + export limit.
- Converge: financial model + scoring matrix → PPA/connection/approvals lock → offtake strategy → GO/NO-GO → close, build, operate, replicate.

## Five investment gates (authoritative)
1. Roof control — Route A owned; Route B 20–30 yr rights with consent and make-good set.
2. Tenant load/offtake — metered daytime load anchors the PPA.
3. DNSP connection/export — firm export figure and augmentation cost in the offer.
4. Approvals — planning, electrical, fire/BESS confirmed per address.
5. Bankable offtake + economics — model scores, EPC priced, offtake strategy locked (see `offtake`).

## Scoring
- Prefer long-lived roofs, high daytime load, strong DNSP capacity.
- A short roof life, weak connection, or short lease kills the site early.
- Size PV to metered daytime load (load-first method), never to roof area. Detail: see `site-qualification`.

## Qualification program
- The HEJ Site Qualification Program (Stages 1–5: longlist 20 → evidence → auto calc → contact → model and pick #1) feeds these gates. Detail: see `site-qualification`.
