## Summary
Name the risks early and price them: offtake term, roof, structure, connection, export, fire, and exit dominate the register. See `site-selection` for the gates that kill weak sites.

## Register
| Risk | Response |
|---|---|
| Tenant churn | Tripartite re-letting terms; no bankable tenor means NO-GO |
| 5-yr lease vs 20–30 yr asset | Match offtake term toward asset life or walk away |
| Missing mortgagee consent | Lender sign-off before close |
| Short roof life / repair dispute | Engineer check + recorded baseline; repair duties in the lease |
| Weak connection / cost unknowns | Lock export and augmentation in the offer; hold a walk-away threshold |
| Export curtailment | Size to onsite load first |
| Negative prices | Keep export as remainder, not strategy |
| Retailer won't pay our price | Lock offtake terms; retailer never obliged to buy at our price |
| Aggregator assumed to be the buyer | Contract the retailer as buyer; scope the aggregator to flexibility only |
| Fire separation | Confirm CFA layout before civil works |
| Unfunded augmentation | Reserve refresh capital at close |
| Make-good dispute | Baseline roof evidence at install |

## Notes
- Amber and rose marks in the flows are risks, not incidents.
- Re-score risks at each gate; close means evidenced, not assumed.
