## Summary
Roof control differs by route — ownership (Route A) vs 20–30 yr lease/licence (Route B, primary) — but both need the tenant bound, the lender consenting, and the exit defined. A 5-year lease cannot carry a 20–30 year asset.

## Route control
- Route A: property ownership carries roof, building, and tenancy together.
- Route B: SPV rooftop lease/licence plus tripartite terms; landlord paid for roof use.
- Either way: mortgagee consent, roof repair/replacement duties, and make-good agreed before close.

## Clause list (14 items, confirm with counsel)
- Parties, premises, and roof area
- Term plus extension options
- Licence fee / landlord payment
- Tripartite tenant obligations
- Access rights for build and O&M
- Structural and roof-condition baseline
- Connection and metering duties
- Insurance and liability split
- Assignment and change of control
- Lender step-in and consent
- Make-good on exit
- Default and cure periods
- Dispute resolution
- Governing law and notices

## Notes
- Tenant churn is the top lease risk: lock tenor or re-letting cover toward the asset life.
- Photograph and record roof condition at install; exit disputes start there.
- This list is a research summary, not drafting advice.

## Open decisions
- Legal sign-off on all 14 clauses before close.
