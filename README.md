# Solar BESS Documentation

Distributed energy platform on commercial and industrial rooftops in Victoria, Australia. Research date: 5 September 2026. Business model realignment: September 2026 (HEJ two-route model).

Two business routes, one energy core. **Route A — property ownership (opportunistic/secondary):** buy the commercial property, control roof + building + tenancy. **Route B — rooftop lease/licence (primary):** 20–30 year roof rights with SPV-owned PV + BESS, replicating Warehouse 001 → 002 → … Preliminary winner: Route B.

Offtake: the Tier-1 tenant onsite PPA anchors revenue; surplus export sells to a licensed retailer / wholesale market participant / aggregator-VPP (under-30 MW General Exemption Order pathway) and settles via AEMO/NEM. The DNSP (AusNet, CitiPower, Jemena, Powercor, United Energy) and VicGrid provide connection and transport only — they never buy energy.
