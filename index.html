Victoria Rooftop Solar + BESS

Documentation hub for a distributed energy platform on Victorian commercial and industrial rooftops — grounded in September 2026 C&I research. Two routes, one energy core: Route A buys the property (opportunistic), Route B secures 20–30 year roof rights with SPV-owned PV + BESS (primary, scalable). The tenant onsite PPA anchors offtake; licensed retailers buy export — the DNSP and VicGrid carry energy, they never buy it. Start with the business picture, then go deep on the engineering.
