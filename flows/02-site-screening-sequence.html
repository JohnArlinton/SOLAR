Site screening: parallel tracks to GO/NO-GO

Due diligence runs as three parallel tracks — property, tenant, energy — not one long sequential list. A candidate site forks into the tracks; evidence converges in the financial model, then PPA/connection/approvals lock, then an explicit Energy Offtake Strategy step, then a single GO/NO-GO investment gate. GO means financial close → construction → operation → replicate (Route B: Warehouse 001 → 002 → …). Every site must answer the same five investment gates: (1) roof control, (2) tenant load/offtake, (3) DNSP connection/export, (4) approvals, (5) bankable offtake + economics.

flowchart TD
  CAND[Candidate site\nRoute A: buy property\nRoute B: 20-30yr roof rights] --> PT[Property track\nroof size + age\nstructure + roof life\nplanning route]
  CAND --> TT[Tenant track\n12-month interval load\nTier-1 offtake\nlease term vs 20-30yr asset]
  CAND --> ET[Energy track\nSunSPOT + shading\nDNSP enquiry\nPV/BESS sizing from load + limit]
  PT --> MOD[Financial model\nscoring matrix\nonsite-first logic]
  TT --> MOD
  ET --> MOD
  MOD --> PPA[PPA + connection + approvals\ntripartite terms\nDNSP offer + export figure\npermit path]
  PPA --> OFF[Energy Offtake Strategy\nwho pays per kWh\ncontract + price + term\nconnection + market rules]
  OFF --> GATE{Investment decision\nGO / NO-GO\nfive gates evidenced?}
  GATE -- GO --> CLOSE[Financial close\nconstruction\noperation\nreplicate 001 to 002]
  GATE -- NO-GO --> KILL[Walk away\nrecord reason]

Steps

#GateDetail
1Gate 1 — roof controlRoute A: owned roof, building, and tenancy. Route B: 20–30 year lease/licence with mortgagee consent, repair/replacement duties, and make-good agreed upfront.
2Gate 2 — tenant load/offtake12 months of metered interval load (8 data requests); Tier-1 tenant anchors the onsite PPA. A 5-year lease cannot carry a 20–30 year asset — tenant churn kills weak sites here.
3Gate 3 — DNSP connection/exportConnection capacity, firm export figure, augmentation cost and timing locked in the offer. The DNSP transports energy only — never a buyer.
4Gate 4 — approvalsPlanning (at or above 1 MW to the minister, planning assumption), electrical, fire/BESS, and environmental path confirmed per address.
5Gate 5 — bankable offtake + economicsFinancial model with the scoring matrix and onsite-first logic; EPC priced against the 8 essentials. Then the Energy Offtake Strategy step: who pays per kWh, what contract/price/term, under what connection and market rules.
6GO / NO-GOAll five gates evidenced — GO means financial close → construction → operation → replicate (Route B: Warehouse 001 → 002 → …). Anything assumed, not evidenced, is NO-GO.

Risks

Thin interval data. Modelled load overstates onsite use. Require metered data before sizing.
Sequential-gate drift. Running ten gates as one long list hides weak offtake until money is spent. Run property/tenant/energy in parallel and force the GO/NO-GO on all five investment gates.
Short lease, long asset. A 5-year tenant lease cannot underwrite 20–30 years of PV + BESS. No bankable offtake term means NO-GO.
