Rooftop Solar + BESS
Business Flows

Two business routes, one energy core. Route A — property ownership: buy the commercial property and control roof, building, and tenancy. Route B — rooftop lease/licence (primary): secure 20–30 year roof rights while the SPV owns the PV and BESS. Both routes run the same core — PV plus optional BESS sized from load profile and export limit, tenant offtake, grid export — traced here from roof rights to settlement, operations, and exit.

01 · ARCHITECTURE

Ecosystem overview

Route A (property ownership) vs Route B (rooftop lease, primary): actors, buildings, PV, ground BESS compound, EMS, meter, network-only DNSP/VicGrid, and NEM settlement.

 02 · SEQUENCE

Site screening

Parallel property/tenant/energy tracks, the five investment gates, the candidate-site fork, and the GO/NO-GO gate before financial close.

 03 · WORKFLOW

Grid connection

Enquiry to energisation across AusNet, CitiPower, Jemena, Powercor, and United Energy. The DNSP transports energy; it never buys it.

 04 · LIFECYCLE

System sizing

Quote to decommission for a 1 MWp / 2 MWh-class pilot with ground-mount BESS.

 05 · SEQUENCE

Install and commission

Build, test, protection, DNSP witness, and energisation.

 06 · DATA FLOW

Dispatch and settlement

PV to onsite tenant PPA first, BESS cycling, export bought by a licensed retailer into the NEM (aggregator monetises flexibility, never buys) — DNSP and VicGrid carry energy, they never buy it.

 07 · WORKFLOW

Operations to exit

Monitoring, O&M, augmentation, decommissioning, community reporting — then replicate: Route B scales Warehouse 001 → 002 → …
