System sizing: quote to decommission

Size every site — Route A or Route B, same energy core — to the roof, the load, and the connection: never to panel price and never to a generic PV-to-BESS ratio. BESS size comes from the tenant load profile plus the DNSP export limit. Reference class: about 1 MWp PV with about 2 MWh BESS. Expect ~1.31 GWh/yr per MWp in Melbourne (~3.6 kWh/kW/day), ~6000–8000 m² of roof per MWp, ground-mounted BESS, and usable capacity below nameplate. All planning assumptions; verify at site.

Risks

Oversizing to export. Negative-price periods punish export-heavy designs. Size to onsite load first.
Generic-ratio sizing. A fixed PV-to-BESS ratio ignores the load profile and the export limit. Size BESS from metered load + offer, per site.
Nameplate confusion. Usable capacity sits below nameplate; degradation needs augmentation budget.
