# Victoria Rooftop Solar + BESS — Business Flows

Two business routes, one energy core. **Route A (property ownership, opportunistic):** buy the commercial property. **Route B (rooftop lease/licence, primary):** 20–30 year roof rights with SPV-owned PV + BESS, replicating Warehouse 001 → 002 → … Seven Mermaid diagrams trace the journey: ecosystem, site screening, grid connection, system sizing, install and commissioning, dispatch and settlement, operations to exit and replicate.
